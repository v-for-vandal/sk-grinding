# Weapons (One-Handed, Two-Handed, Archery)

> Part of the design: [`design-v2.md`](../../design-v2.md) (was §7.1). Bare `§N` references point to design-v2.md. Index of skills: [`skills/README.md`](../README.md).

**Sub-skills:**

| Parent | Sub-skills |
|---|---|
| One-Handed | `1h-sword` (sword, katana, rapier, saber, scimitar), `1h-short-sword` (shortsword, wakizashi), `1h-dagger` (dagger, claw, tanto, sai…), `1h-mace` (mace, club, flail, whip), `1h-war-axe` (war axe, hatchet, pickaxe), `1h-spear` (1H spear, javelin, 1H pike/halberd/glaive/lance/trident), `1h-scythe` (1H warscythe, sickle), `1h-unarmed` (fists, cestus), `1h-other` |
| Two-Handed | `2h-sword` (greatsword, 2H katana), `2h-battleaxe`, `2h-warhammer` (warhammer, giant clubs), `2h-polearm` (halberd, pike, glaive, lance, 2H spear, trident), `2h-quarterstaff` (quarterstaff, bladestaff), `2h-scythe`, `2h-other` |
| Archery | `archery-light-bow`, `archery-heavy-bow`, `archery-light-crossbow`, `archery-heavy-crossbow`, `archery-other` (guns, blowguns, slingshots) |
| One-Handed (concept) | `dual-wield`, `with-shield`, `with-magic` |

The list is decided (Q-WPN-1). There is no `1h-quarterstaff`: LoreRim has no real one-handed
quarterstaff (one test record), so OCF `Quarterstaff1H` goes to `1h-other`.

- **Concept sub-skills** (`dual-wield`, `with-shield`, `with-magic`) have `k = 0`: they give
  nothing to the main skill (§4.4.1). They exist for their effects and perks.

- XP base amount per hit is the vanilla (Lorerim/Requiem) amount, captured from the suppressed
  vanilla award (see §14 technical notes), and then routed to sub-skills.
- **Weapon class is determined by keywords only** (decided). A KID config gives every weapon
  exactly one `SPO_WeapClass_*` keyword from vanilla, Requiem, OCF, Animated Armoury, Spear
  Mechanic and other type keywords, referenced by FormID (several plugins define different
  keywords with the same EditorID). The DLL checks only these keywords; no animation-type checks.
  Config: [`config/kid/SPO_WeaponClass_KID.ini`](../../config/kid/SPO_WeaponClass_KID.ini), generated by
  [`scripts/gen_kid_weapons.py`](../../scripts/gen_kid_weapons.py) (priority order, per-form fixes,
  static check). Details: [`refs/lorerim/weapons.md`](../../refs/lorerim/weapons.md).
  - The parent main skill comes from the class, not from the weapon's trained skill (e.g. a gun
    with a one-handed animation trains `archery-other`).
  - Light vs heavy bows and crossbows come from Requiem keywords (decided, Q-WPN-2):
    `REQ_WeaponType_BowLight/BowHeavy` (`9F9915`/`9F9914:Requiem.esp`),
    `REQ_WeaponType_CrossbowLight/CrossbowHeavy` (`9F7179`/`899DBE:Requiem.esp`), never weight.
    An unmarked bow or crossbow is light.
  - The sub-skill registry and the co-save are keyed by sub-skill string id (Q-WPN-10).
  - Bound weapons train both their weapon sub-skill and `conjure-bound` (decided, Q-WPN-6).
  - A weapon without any class keyword goes to `1h-other`, `2h-other` or `archery-other` by
    its hand (Q-WPN-11, decided). This fallback is the only place where the DLL looks at the
    weapon's animation type, and it is logged once per weapon.
  - **Beast forms give no weapon XP** (decided, Q-WPN-3): werewolf and vampire lord attacks
    train no weapon sub-skill and are excluded from the fallback above (beast forms have their
    own perk trees). Unarmed is `1h-unarmed` under One-Handed (the LoreRim One-Handed tree has
    Hand to Hand, Grappling, Unarmed Defense, Unarmed Stance); claw *weapons* go to `1h-dagger`.
- Staves and wands are cast, not used in melee, so they are not weapons here (see [wands and staves](../enchanting/design.md)).

**Continuous effects** (curve of §5.7; values are % changes of the player's weapon damage). **Armor
penetration is not touched** (decided): Requiem's own penetration (`OneHandedModifier` etc.,
from its perks) stays as it is.

- **Weapon sub-skills** (every `1h-*`, `2h-*`, `archery-*`): the sub-skill of the weapon that
  makes the attack.

  | | `pen` at 0 | `P` | `M` | `h` | at 75 | at 100 | at 150 | at 200 | at 500 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | damage | −20 | 50 | 45 | 40 | +17.3 | **+25.0** | +32.1 | **+35.5** | +41.3 |

- **`dual-wield`** (applies while a weapon is in each hand). Each pair of weapon classes has a
  checkpoint `C` (table below).
  - **Penalty:** the effective level of the attacking weapon's sub-skill is `current × factor`,
    factor 0.75 at `dual-wield` 0, linearly to 1.0 at `C` (§5.7).
  - **Bonus:** above `C`, a weapon-damage multiplier with `M = 45`, `h = 25` (+30% at `C + 50`,
    +36% at `C + 100`, limit +45%).

  Hand tiers: **D** = `1h-dagger`; **S** = `1h-short-sword`; **M** = `1h-sword`, `1h-mace`,
  `1h-war-axe`, `1h-scythe`, `1h-other`; **Sp** = `1h-spear`.

  | Pair | D | S | M | Sp |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | **D** | 20 | 30 | 40 | 50 |
  | **S** | 30 | 30 | 40 | 50 |
  | **M** | 40 | 40 | 75 | 90 |
  | **Sp** | 50 | 50 | 90 | 100 |

  A fist (`1h-unarmed`) in either hand is never dual-wielding: no penalty, no bonus, no
  `dual-wield` XP (Q-WPN-12).

- **`with-shield`** (weapon attacks while a shield is equipped; blocking itself is never
  penalized). **Penalty:** effective level of the weapon sub-skill = `current × factor`, factor
  0.75 at `with-shield` 0, linearly to 1.0 at 20 (at 40 for `1h-spear`). No bonus: after the
  checkpoint the sub-skill only unlocks perks (decided, Q-WPN-14). Effect on weapon damage at
  `with-shield` 0:

  | weapon current | 0 | 20 | 40 | 50 | 75 | 100 | 150 | 200 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | without shield, % | −20 | −12 | −4 | 0 | +17.3 | +25 | +32.1 | +35.5 |
  | ×0.75 level, % | −20 | −14 | −8 | −5 | +6.1 | +17.3 | +27.4 | +32.1 |
  | difference, pp | 0 | −2 | −4 | −5 | **−11.2** | −7.7 | −4.7 | −3.4 |

  The penalty is largest just above the checkpoint (≈ 75) and small at both ends: a beginner who
  trains sword and shield together grows evenly, an experienced swordsman who takes a shield for
  the first time loses the most.
- **`with-magic`** (a weapon in one hand, a spell, staff or wand in the other). **Penalty:**
  effective level of both the weapon sub-skill and the spell's magic sub-skill =
  `current × factor`, factor 0.75 at `with-magic` 0, linearly to 1.0 at 45. No bonus: after 45
  the sub-skill only unlocks perks.
  - Only our sub-skills' effects are affected; `current`, perk thresholds and the main skill
    are not (Q-WPN-15, decided).

- **Implementation** (Q-DYN-9, option 3): damage multipliers in a DLL hook on the hit /
  damage calculation, applied once per hit after vanilla and Requiem perks; weapon class from
  `SPO_WeapClass_*` of the attacking weapon. The hook sees the weapon (and hand) of each hit, so
  dual-wielding two different classes needs nothing special. (Armor penetration was dropped
  partly because Requiem keeps it in one actor value shared by both hands.)

**Window rules** (window length `windowSec = 10`, configurable):

- An event qualifies if a **complementary** event happened within the last `windowSec` seconds
  (looking back only). No one-to-one pairing: every qualifying event grants XP.
- Events (decided, Q-WPN-4): a **weapon hit** is a landed hit (not a swing); a **spell cast** is
  any cast that grants XP to its own magic sub-skill. A qualifying event gives the concept
  sub-skill the same XP that the event gives to its own sub-skill (weapon or magic).
  - Note: vanilla raw XP is not on one scale across skills: a weapon hit gives its base damage
    (an iron sword: 7), a spell cast its base magicka cost (Flames 14, Fireball 133) (see
    [`refs/vanilla-skill-xp.md`](../../refs/vanilla-skill-xp.md)). So the XP is taken **after** our
    per-skill base multipliers (Q-XP-9), which are set so that comparable activity gives
    comparable sub-skill XP.
  Example for `with-magic`: hit–spell–hit–hit–spell within 10 s → 4 awards (the first hit has
  nothing before it).
- `with-magic`: complementary pair = (weapon hit, spell cast). Weapon hit qualifies if a spell
  was cast in the window; spell cast qualifies if there was a weapon hit in the window.
- `dual-wield`: complementary pair = (main-hand attack, off-hand attack).
- `with-shield`: weapon hit with a shield equipped grants `with-shield` XP × **1.0** if a block
  happened in the window, otherwise × **0.5** (coefficients configurable). Symmetric (decided,
  Q-WPN-5): a block with a weapon hit in the window also grants `with-shield` XP.

**Accuracy** (decided; mechanics in [`refs/aim-perturbation.md`](../../refs/aim-perturbation.md)):

- **Archery** (Q-WPN-7): low archery sub-skills make arrows and bolts less accurate: direction
  noise at launch, `σ` decreasing with the level, plus a **visible aim sway** while drawing.
- **Melee** (Q-WPN-8): low melee sub-skills cause **glancing hits**: with a chance that falls with
  the sub-skill level, a hit deals reduced damage (`glancingDamage`, e.g. ×0.5) and does not
  stagger. No full misses. Chance curve and values: config (placeholder: 30% at 0, 0 at 50).
